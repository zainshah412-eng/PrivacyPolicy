Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

FlowSort is a free puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

FlowSort itself does not collect any personally identifiable information. However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect data as described below.

2. Advertising

FlowSort uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, operating system, and language
  • App usage and interaction data

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Analytics

FlowSort uses Firebase Analytics by Google to understand how players interact with the game. This may include:

  • Levels played and completed
  • Session duration and frequency
  • Device type and operating system version
  • App crashes and performance data (via Firebase Crashlytics)

This data is collected anonymously and is used solely to improve the game experience. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

4. Data Storage

Game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Level completion and star ratings
  • Coin balance
  • Sound and haptics settings
  • Daily reward streak

This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

FlowSort is rated Everyone and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Advertising shown to users under 13 is non-personalized in compliance with Google's families policy.

6. Permissions

FlowSort requests the following device permissions:

  • Internet — Required to load advertisements and analytics
  • Network State — To check connectivity before loading ads
  • Vibration — For optional haptic feedback during gameplay

No camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions are requested.

7. Third-Party Services

FlowSort integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Privacy Policy

8. Data Retention

Local game data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data retained by Google is subject to Google's own data retention policies.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt out of personalized advertising via device settings
  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the app
  • Disable Firebase Analytics data collection by turning off Usage & Diagnostics in your device settings

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
