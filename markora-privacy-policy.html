Privacy Policy

Markora: Watermark Photos · Zavi Studio
Last updated: 10 September 2026

The short version: Markora watermarks your photos entirely on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app needs no account and no sign-in. The only data that leaves your device is advertising and crash/usage information collected by Google, described below.

1. Who we are

Markora: Watermark Photos ("Markora", "the app") is published by Zavi Studio ("we", "us"). You can reach us at zavistudioinfo@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

2. Your photos stay on your device

Markora adds watermarks using your phone's own processing. We do not upload, copy, transmit, view, or store your photos, your watermark text, your logos, or your signatures. There is no Markora server that receives your images, because there is no Markora server at all.

  • You choose which photos to open through Android's system photo picker. Markora only ever reads the photos you pick.
  • Watermarked copies are written to Pictures/Markora in your device's gallery, where you control them like any other photo.
  • Markora works with no internet connection. Only the advertising and analytics described below require one.

3. What Markora stores on your device

The following is kept in Markora's private storage on your phone and is not sent anywhere:

  • Watermark templates you save, including their text, colours and layout.
  • Logo and signature images you add, copied into private storage so a saved template keeps working later.
  • Your export settings (format, quality, size, file naming).
  • A list of the files Markora has exported, so the Creations tab can show your history. This is a list of references, not copies of the images.
  • Your app preferences, such as theme and analytics choice.

Uninstalling Markora deletes all of this. Photos you already exported stay in your gallery, because they belong to you.

4. Data collected by Google

Markora is free and supported by advertising. Two Google services collect information from the app:

Google AdMob (advertising)

AdMob serves the banner and full-screen ads in Markora and receives device identifiers, including your advertising ID, along with technical information such as device type, operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. This is used to select and measure ads and to detect fraud. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting)

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collect anonymous usage events (for example, that an export started or completed) and crash diagnostics, so we can find and fix problems. These reports never contain your photos or the contents of your watermarks. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Analytics.

5. Advertising consent

Where the law requires a choice — including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — Markora asks for your consent before requesting personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network state — required only for advertising and crash reporting. Watermarking itself never uses it.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — needed to write your watermarked photos into the public Pictures folder. On Android 10 and newer, Markora needs no storage permission at all.

Markora does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or photo-library permissions. Reading the photos you pick is handled by Android's photo picker, which grants access only to the photos you selected.

7. Children

Markora is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will act on it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Turn analytics off in Settings.
  • Change your advertising consent in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.
  • Delete everything Markora stores by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because Markora holds no account and no server-side data about you, requests of this kind generally concern data held by Google; contact us and we will help you direct them.

9. Data retention

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Markora handles data, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
