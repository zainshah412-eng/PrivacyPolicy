The firewall uses Android’s VpnService to block the apps you select, entirely on your device. Please note:

  • Android, your device maker or the system may stop the firewall, for example under battery optimisation, after updates or when memory is low. Set the App’s battery usage to “Unrestricted” for best reliability.

  • Blocking an app can stop its messages, notifications, sync or updates. You choose what to block and are responsible for that choice.
  • Automatic blocking at your data limit, roaming mode and Wi-Fi blocking depend on the same limits above.

Because of these limits, we do not guarantee that every connection from a blocked app will be stopped.
