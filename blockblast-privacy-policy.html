Privacy Policy for Block Drop Puzzle

Effective date: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Block Drop Puzzle ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Block Drop Puzzle is a block-clearing puzzle game for Android. It does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, or any other directly-identifying information.

1. Information We Collect

Stored only on your device

Your game scores, best scores, and level/campaign progress (including stars earned per level) are stored locally on your device using standard Android storage (Room database and DataStore preferences). This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate, and it is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

Automatically collected via third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect certain technical and usage information automatically. We do not control what these services collect beyond the settings we configure; please review each provider's own privacy policy for full details.

Service Purpose Data involved
Google AdMob Displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads that keep the App free to play Advertising ID, device/app information, ad interaction data. May include personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your device and region settings.
Firebase Analytics Understand feature usage (e.g. levels played, game mode selected) to improve the App App usage events, device model, OS version, approximate location derived from IP address
Firebase Crashlytics Detect and fix crashes and bugs Crash logs, stack traces, device model, OS version

Google's own privacy practices for these services are described at policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display ads that fund free access to the App
  • To understand which features and levels are used, so we can improve the game
  • To detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to operating and improving the App.

3. Children's Privacy

Block Drop Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

4. Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device's Google Settings → Ads menu, or by resetting/limiting your Advertising ID.
  • Local data: You can clear your locally stored scores and progress at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Block Drop Puzzle → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics collection can be limited using your device's ad and diagnostics privacy settings.

5. Data Security

We rely on the security practices of Google's Firebase and AdMob platforms for any data those services process. Locally stored game data stays within your device's app-private storage, which is sandboxed by the Android operating system.

6. Data Retention

Locally stored data persists only until you clear app data or uninstall the App. Data collected by Firebase and AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention policies, linked above.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
