CastNova: Cast & Mirror to TV

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Developer: Zavi Studio · Package: com.zavistudio.castnova

In short: CastNova works on your own Wi‑Fi. There is no account and no CastNova cloud. Your photos, videos, music and screen go straight from your phone to the TV or browser you choose on your local network. We never upload them to our servers. We don't have any.

1. Casting and mirroring stay on your network

To cast, CastNova runs a small temporary web server on your phone. Only the TV or browser you connect can use it. Each item gets an unguessable link, and a browser must enter a PIN or a one-time code before it receives anything. This traffic stays inside your local network and uses plain HTTP there, as most casting apps do, so use CastNova on networks you trust. The server stops when you stop casting.

2. Screen and sound

Screen mirroring starts only after you allow it in Android's own screen-sharing prompt, and it runs only while mirroring is on. CastNova sends your screen, and the sound your apps play, only to the TV or browser you connected. Android requires the "Record audio" permission to capture that app sound. CastNova never uses the microphone. Nothing is recorded or saved.

3. Video conversion

If your TV can't play a video's format, CastNova can convert it to one your TV supports. This happens on your phone. The converted copy is kept in the app's temporary storage only while you cast it and is deleted when casting ends.

4. Information stored on your phone

  • Your settings, such as theme and mirroring quality.
  • Recently used TVs, so they appear first next time.
  • Where you stopped a video, so you can resume it.
  • TV pairing keys. When you use "Open on TV", your Samsung or LG TV gives CastNova a key after you press Allow on the TV. It is used only to ask that TV to open CastNova's page.

This information never leaves your phone. TV pairing keys are excluded from backups. Uninstalling CastNova deletes all of it.

5. Data handled by Google services

CastNova uses these Google services. Each one processes data under Google's own policies.

  • Ads (Google AdMob). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information. Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, CastNova asks for your consent first using Google's consent form. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Usage events, such as "cast started" or which screen was opened, together with device and app information. Never the content you cast, file names or addresses on your network. You can turn analytics off in Settings.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the app crashes, it sends the device model, Android version and technical details of the error. We use these only to fix bugs.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing). If CastNova offers an in-app purchase, Google Play handles payment. We never see your payment details.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or the content of your photos, videos, music or screen. CastNova does not sell personal data and does not share it with anyone other than the Google services listed above.

8. Your choices

  • Turn analytics off in Settings → Usage analytics.
  • Change or withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy options, where available.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings.
  • Uninstall CastNova to delete everything it stored on your phone.

9. Data retention and security

CastNova itself keeps no data off your phone. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google under Firebase's retention settings. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit.

10. Children

CastNova is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.
