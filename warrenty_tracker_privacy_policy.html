Privacy Policy

Warranty & Receipt Tracker  ·  Last updated: 8 August 2026

In short: Warranty & Receipt Tracker works entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your receipts, warranties, and records never leave your phone.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how the Warranty & Receipt Tracker Android application (package com.zavistudio.warrantytracker, the “App”) handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts and requires no sign-up. All content you create — item names, prices, notes, warranty dates, categories, and scanned receipt images — is stored only in the App’s private storage on your device.

The App does not request the INTERNET permission and has no server or backend. Because there is no network component, your data is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

3. Permissions we use

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+): used only to remind you locally before a warranty is due to expire. Reminders are generated on your device; nothing is sent externally. You can decline this permission and the App still works.

Receipt scanning: the App uses the on-device Google Play Services document scanner (ML Kit) to let you capture a receipt. The scan happens on your device and the resulting image is saved to the App’s private storage. The App itself does not hold a camera permission and does not send images anywhere.

4. How your data is stored

All data is stored locally in the App’s private, sandboxed storage (an on-device database and internal image files). It is not accessible to other apps. Optional exports you create (CSV or PDF) are saved only where you choose to save or share them.

5. Data sharing & selling

We do not share, rent, or sell your data to anyone. Data only leaves the App when you explicitly choose to export or share it (for example, sharing a PDF or CSV through another app you select). What happens after that is governed by the app or service you share it with.

6. Third-party services

The App does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third-party component is Google Play Services’ on-device document scanner used for receipt capture, which processes the image locally on your device.

7. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. As no data is collected, it is safe for general audiences.

8. Your control over your data

You are in full control. You can edit or delete any item at any time within the App. To remove all data, clear the App’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the App — this permanently deletes everything stored on the device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
