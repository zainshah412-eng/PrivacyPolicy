Privacy Policy

Electricity Bill Checker

Last updated: May 2025

Summary: This app does not collect, store, or share your personal data. We do not require sign-up or login. Your bill reference numbers are stored locally on your device only.
1

Introduction

Electricity Bill Checker ("the App") is a utility application that allows users in Pakistan to check their electricity bills from various DISCOs (Distribution Companies) including LESCO, MEPCO, GEPCO, FESCO, IESCO, PESCO, QESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, TESCO, KE, HAZECO, and AJK.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2

Information we collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • No name, email address, or phone number is collected
  • No account registration or login is required
  • No location data is collected or tracked
  • Bill reference numbers you enter are stored locally on your device only
No sign-up required No personal data collected Local storage only
3

How the app works

The App functions as a WebView-based interface. When you enter a bill reference number, the App opens the official DISCO website in a WebView to display your bill. We do not intercept, store, or transmit any data you enter or any data returned from those official websites.

Saved bills are stored using Android SharedPreferences — entirely on your local device — and are never uploaded to any server.

4

Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect anonymised usage data according to their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob — Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out via your device's ad settings.

policies.google.com/privacy →

Google Firebase — Used for app stability and crash reporting. Firebase may collect anonymised diagnostic data.

firebase.google.com/support/privacy →

Official DISCO Websites — When viewing a bill, the App loads the official electricity company website. That website's own privacy policy applies to any data exchanged with it.

5

Advertising

The App is free and supported by Google AdMob advertisements. AdMob may use advertising identifiers (GAID) on your device to show personalised ads. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can disable personalised ads from your Android device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6

Data storage & security

All data saved within the App (such as saved bill references) is stored locally on your Android device using Android's SharedPreferences API. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing the App's data via Android Settings
  • Is permanently removed when the App is uninstalled
7

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The App is a general utility tool and is not directed at children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

8

Internet permission

The App requires internet access (android.permission.INTERNET) solely to load official DISCO websites in the WebView and to serve advertisements. No other network calls are made by the App itself.
