01 Overview

Device Doctor AI ("the app") is a system diagnostics utility developed by Zavi Studio that monitors CPU, RAM, battery, storage, and network health on your Android device, and produces an on-device health score.

The app is built to work without a server backend. There is no sign-up, no user profile, and no account system. Diagnostic data the app reads (battery level, RAM usage, storage breakdown, network type, etc.) is processed locally on your device to generate the readings, history, and health score you see in the app — it is not transmitted to Zavi Studio or stored on any server we operate.

04 Advertising (AdMob)

The free version of Device Doctor AI shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process the advertising identifier, coarse device/network information, and interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use this data consistent with Google's own policies.

Zavi Studio does not receive or store the data AdMob collects — it is processed directly by Google. You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized ads, from your device's Google Settings app under Ads.

For details on how Google handles this data, see Google's own Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchasing the premium tier (where available) removes ads from the app.

05 Storage & retention

Diagnostic readings and history (battery, RAM, storage, health score trends) are stored in a local database on your device only, using Android's Room persistence library. This data:

  • Never leaves your device or reaches Zavi Studio servers — we don't operate a backend for this app.
  • Is retained on-device for the history window shown in Settings, and is deleted automatically as older entries roll off, or immediately if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Is included in Android's standard app backup (if enabled on your device) since the app does not disable system backup — you can turn this off in your device's system backup settings.

06 Home screen widgets

Device Doctor AI offers Battery, RAM, and Health Score home-screen widgets. Widgets read the same on-device diagnostics described in §2 to display live values and do not introduce any additional data collection.

07 Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share diagnostic data, installed-app lists, or usage data with third parties, brokers, or advertisers. The only outbound data flow is the advertising identifier passed to Google AdMob as described in §4, which is required for the ad-supported free tier to function.

08 Children's privacy

Device Doctor AI is a general-audience system utility not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details in §11 and we will address it.

09 Security

Because diagnostic data stays on-device rather than being transmitted to a server, the primary safeguard is the security of your own device (screen lock, OS updates, and Android's app sandboxing). We follow standard Android development practices to avoid exposing this data to other apps.

10 Your choices

  • Revoke permissions anytime from Android Settings → Apps → Device Doctor AI → Permissions. Some features (e.g. Wi-Fi name display, largest-files scan) will stop working without their corresponding permission.
  • Delete your data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app — since all history is local, this permanently removes it.
  • Opt out of ad personalization via your device's Google Settings → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

11 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add crash reporting, analytics, or a cloud backup feature in a future update — we'll update the "Effective" date at the top of this page and, for material changes, note it in the app's release notes.
