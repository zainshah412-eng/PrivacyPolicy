Data Usage Monitor Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · App: Data Usage Monitor (com.scriptupsolutions.appusagemonitor) · Developer: Zavi Studio

In short: Data Usage Monitor has no accounts and we (Zavi Studio) run no servers. Your screen time, data usage, block lists and settings are processed and stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. The firewall is a local VPN that never routes your traffic anywhere. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device data as described below.

This policy explains what information is handled when you use the Data Usage Monitor Android app (the “App”), published by Zavi Studio (“we”, “us”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your phone

To show your usage and run its features, the App reads and keeps the following locally on your device:

  • Screen time and app usage, read from Android’s usage statistics (UsageStatsManager), after you grant Usage access
  • Mobile and Wi-Fi data usage per app, read from Android’s network statistics (NetworkStatsManager)
  • The list of installed apps that have a launcher icon, so you can see their usage and choose which to block
  • Your settings: block lists, data plan and limits, theme, alerts, data meter and roaming options

This information is never uploaded to us or to any server we run. It is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

2. Mobile Data Firewall (VpnService)

The firewall uses Android’s VpnService API to block internet access for the apps you choose. This is the only Android API that allows per-app blocking without root.

  • The VPN is created locally on your phone; your traffic is never routed to any external server
  • Traffic from blocked apps is simply dropped on the device; other apps connect normally
  • The App does not read, inspect, log or store the content of any traffic, browsing history or passwords
  • The firewall only starts when you turn it on, and a notification is shown while it is running
  • Optional features — Wi-Fi blocking, roaming mode, automatic blocking at your data limit and the prompt for newly installed apps — work the same way, entirely on your phone

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the App. Anywhere, you can limit ad personalisation or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play in-app review

The App may ask you to rate it using Google Play’s in-app review. That dialog is provided by Google Play; we do not receive your rating, review or account details through it.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. It does not use the “all apps” package-visibility permission; it only sees apps that have a launcher icon.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. On-device data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings. Data collected by Google is retained under Google’s policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

9. Security

Data saved by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The new version will be posted on this page and shown in the App with an updated effective date.
