Privacy Policy — SmartCleaner

Developer: Zavi Studio  •  Package: com.scriptupsolutions.smartcleaner
Effective date: July 3, 2026  •  Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SmartCleaner ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. SmartCleaner is a storage-management utility that helps you find and remove junk files, duplicate files, large files, screenshots, leftover APKs, and app cache to free up space on your device.

Short version: SmartCleaner does not require an account, does not collect or transmit your personal files, photos, or documents to us or to any server, and does not sell your data. All file scanning and cleaning happens entirely on your device. The only data leaving your device is limited technical/advertising data handled by Google AdMob, described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

SmartCleaner does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account/profile information (the App has no sign-in or account system)
  • The contents of your photos, videos, documents, or downloaded files
  • File contents are never uploaded anywhere — scanning, hashing (for duplicate detection), and deletion all happen locally on your device
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Contacts, messages, or call logs

2. Information Processed On-Device

To provide its core features, SmartCleaner reads certain file metadata (name, size, file path, last-modified date, and MIME type) via the Android MediaStore and file system APIs. This metadata is processed and stored only locally on your device using Room (SQLite) and DataStore, to:

  • Display scan results (junk, duplicate, large, screenshot, and APK files)
  • Remember your last scan results and settings
  • Track your lifetime storage freed and auto-clean schedule

None of this metadata is sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored data.

3. Permissions Used and Why

PermissionPurpose
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (legacy) Read media file metadata (name, size, date) to detect junk, duplicate, large, and screenshot files. File contents are never read beyond what's needed to compute a duplicate-detection hash on-device.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Core to the App's file-management function: used to (a) list installed apps and their external cache sizes for the Cache Cleaner feature, and (b) detect leftover ("residual") folders belonging to apps you've already uninstalled.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows a notification when a scheduled background auto-clean completes, if you've enabled that feature in Settings.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Allow the optional scheduled auto-clean feature to run reliably in the background, in compliance with Android's background execution limits.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required by Google AdMob to serve ads (see Section 4). SmartCleaner itself makes no other network calls.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

SmartCleaner shows banner ads through Google AdMob to keep the App free. AdMob may collect and process device and usage information — such as your Advertising ID, IP address, device type, and general ad-interaction data — to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and Google acts as an independent controller for this data.

You can control ad personalization and reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. For details on how Google collects and uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's own Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share any data with third parties except as described in Section 4 (Google AdMob), which is required for the App's ad-supported free model.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data (scan history, settings, preferences) is stored locally in Room/DataStore on your device and is automatically and permanently deleted when you uninstall the App. There is no server-side account or backup of this data for us to retain.

7. Children's Privacy

SmartCleaner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

8. Security

Since SmartCleaner does not transmit your personal files or file metadata to any server, there is no server-side data store for us to secure. Locally stored app data is sandboxed by Android's standard app-data protections.

9. Your Choices

  • You can grant or revoke storage/media and notification permissions at any time via Android Settings → Apps → SmartCleaner → Permissions.
  • You can disable the scheduled auto-clean feature at any time from within the App's Settings screen.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization from your device's system settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.
