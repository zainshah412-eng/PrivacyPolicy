Privacy Policy

AudioFuse: MP3 Merger & Cutter · Published by Zavi Studio · Effective 10 September 2026

AudioFuse is an offline audio editor. It is built so that your audio files never leave your device. This policy explains what little information the app does handle, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up. We do not know who you are.
  • Your audio and video files are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • The app works without an internet connection.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics (you can turn this off) and crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads.

1. Information we do not collect

AudioFuse does not collect, store, or transmit: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or the contents or names of the audio and video files you edit. Files you open are read only to perform the edit you requested, and the exported result is written to your device's Music/AudioFuse folder. Your original files are never modified.

3. Permissions the app requests

  • Read audio / open files — only through the Android file picker, and only for the files you choose. The app cannot browse your storage on its own.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — to show export progress and let you cancel a running export. Optional.
  • Storage (Android 9 and lower only) — to save exported files to the Music folder.
  • Internet — used only by the analytics, crash-reporting and advertising services above. Editing and exporting never use the network.
  • Foreground service — keeps a long export running if you switch apps. It shows a persistent progress notification while active.

4. Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no account data, there is nothing for us to delete about you personally. Exported files stay on your device until you delete them (from the app's My Creations screen or any file manager). Temporary working files are stored in the app's cache and removed automatically after each export; you can also clear them in Settings → Clear temporary files. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to Firebase's default retention periods (up to 14 months for analytics; 90 days for crash reports). To request deletion of analytics or crash data tied to your installation, email us and include the app name and, if possible, your device model and approximate install date.

5. Children

AudioFuse is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

All communication with the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Since your files are never transmitted, they are exactly as safe as the rest of the data on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store "What's new" section.
