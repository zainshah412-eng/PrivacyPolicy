Privacy Policy

Magnifier Pro

Effective date: 8 August 2026  •  Last updated: 8 August 2026

The short version

  • Magnifier Pro’s core features (magnifying, text recognition, read-aloud) run entirely on your device. Camera frames are processed live and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • We do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Images are saved only when you choose to save them, and they stay on your device / in your gallery.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and may use Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics. These third parties may collect device and usage data as described below. You can control ad personalization and turn analytics off.

1. Who we are

This app, Magnifier Pro (package com.zavistudio.magnifier), is developed and published by zavistudio (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app and its third-party services collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By downloading or using Magnifier Pro, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information the app collects

Camera and images

Magnifier Pro uses your device camera to magnify text and objects and to recognize text (OCR). This processing happens locally on your device using Google’s on-device ML Kit. Live camera frames are not stored and not transmitted. A photo is saved only when you tap “Save”, in which case it is written to your device’s gallery and a reference is kept in the app’s local history on your device. You can delete saved captures at any time from within the app or your gallery.

Personal information

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or any account credentials. Magnifier Pro has no login or user account.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To provide and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information, which can include your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, general (coarse) location, device type, and ad interaction data. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form so you can choose whether personalized ads are used. See Google’s policies linked in Section 5.

Analytics and crash reporting (optional)

If enabled by the developer, the app may use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to diagnose crashes and stability issues. This may include aggregated, pseudonymous data such as app events (e.g. screen opened, feature used), device model, OS version, app version, language, and crash logs. This data is used only to improve the app. You can turn off usage analytics at any time in Settings → Usage analytics.

  • To provide the app’s features (magnification, text recognition, read-aloud, saving and sharing images).
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob.
  • To understand feature usage and fix crashes and bugs (if analytics/crash reporting is enabled).
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information.

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — How Google uses data · Google Privacy Policy

6. Your choices and controls

  • Ad personalization: where the consent form is shown, you can change your choice at any time via the privacy options in the app (when available for your region).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Analytics: turn off Usage analytics in the app’s Settings screen.
  • Camera permission: revoke it any time in your device’s app settings (the app will simply stop magnifying until re-granted).
  • Delete your data: delete saved captures in the app or your gallery, and uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

7. Data retention

Images and capture history you create are stored on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Advertising and analytics data handled by Google is retained according to Google’s own retention policies (see the links in Section 5).

8. Children’s privacy

Magnifier Pro is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it.

9. Security

Because core processing happens on your device and we do not operate user accounts, your captured images stay under your control. Data transmitted to Google’s advertising and analytics services is protected in transit using industry-standard encryption. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
