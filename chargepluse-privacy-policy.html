Privacy Policy

ChargePulse — Battery Alarm · Zavi Studio · Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ChargePulse — Battery Alarm app ("ChargePulse", "the app") published by Zavi Studio ("we", "us") handles information when you use it on your Android device.

In short
  • No account or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your battery readings and charging history are processed and stored only on your device. We never upload them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage analytics and crash reports. These Google services collect limited device data as described below.
  • You can turn off usage analytics and manage ad consent in Settings → Privacy.

1. Information processed on your device

To provide battery alarms and charging statistics, ChargePulse reads information Android reports about your battery: charge level, charging state and charger type, battery temperature, voltage and current (where your device reports them).

From these readings the app keeps a local history of charging sessions (start and end time, start and end level, duration, temperatures). Your alert settings and app preferences are also stored locally.

  • This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party.
  • Charging history is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. App preferences (such as alert thresholds and theme) may be included in your own Android backup if you have backup enabled; that backup is managed by Google under your Google account.
  • You can delete charging history at any time in the app (History → Clear charging history). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

3. Your choices

  • Usage analytics: turn off Settings → Usage analytics at any time.
  • Ad consent: where consent is required, you can review or change your choice in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Personalized ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Notifications and other permissions: can be changed in your device's system settings.

5. Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use them only to respond and to improve the app, and delete them on request. The app itself does not send us any data.

6. Data retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you clear it in the app or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google services is retained according to Google's policies. To request deletion of any emails we hold, contact us at the address below.

7. Security

Local data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Communication by the third-party services above uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Children

ChargePulse is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
