Introduction

VoltGuard ("we", "our", or "the app") is a battery health monitoring application developed by ScriptUp Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what rights you have as a user.

By installing or using VoltGuard, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

VoltGuard is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app reads battery information from your device solely to display it to you. This information is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared.

Information the App Reads

VoltGuard reads the following information from your device to provide its features. All of this information is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted externally.

Data Type Purpose Stored?
Battery level (%) Display current charge, trigger charge alarms Locally in app database
Battery voltage (mV) Display real-time voltage on dashboard Locally in app database
Battery temperature (°C) Display temperature, show color-coded alerts Locally in app database
Charging current (mA) Show charge speed, live current graph Locally in app database
Battery health status Show health grade (A/B/C/D/F) Locally in app database
Battery capacity (mAh) Calculate wear level and health percentage Locally in app database

App foreground usage time Show per-app battery consumption (Usage screen) Not stored — fetched on demand
Screen on/off time Calculate deep sleep percentage Not stored — calculated on demand
Charging session data Session history, habit score, health trend Locally in app database

Permissions Explained

VoltGuard requests the following Android permissions. Each is required for a specific feature and is not used beyond that purpose.

Permission Why It's Needed
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Keeps battery monitoring active in the background and shows the persistent status notification.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Required by Android 14+ to declare battery monitoring as a special use foreground service.
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS Required to show per-app battery usage on the Usage screen. Must be granted manually by the user in Settings. Optional — all other features work without it.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Required for the Gaming Mode floating overlay that shows battery % and temperature over games.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required on Android 13+ to show charge alarm notifications and the persistent monitoring notification.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Required for the Smart Charge Scheduler to notify you at a precise time when to plug in your charger.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restarts the battery monitoring service automatically after the device is rebooted.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents the CPU from sleeping during critical battery monitoring tasks.
VIBRATE Vibrates the device when a charge alarm is triggered.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads. Not used by VoltGuard itself for any data collection.
INTERNET Used by Google AdMob to load advertisements. VoltGuard does not make any network requests itself.
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Used on Android 9 and below only to export battery history as a CSV file. Only triggered by user action.

Data Storage

VoltGuard stores the following data locally on your device using a private Room (SQLite) database and Android DataStore. This data cannot be accessed by other apps.

  • Charging session history (start/end time, level, current, energy added)
  • Battery health records (capacity, wear level, cycle count over time)
  • Battery snapshots for trend charts (level, voltage, temperature, timestamp)
  • Gaming session records (per-game battery drain)
  • User preferences (theme, language, alarm settings, notification style)
All stored data lives in your device's private app storage. It is deleted automatically when you uninstall VoltGuard. You can also clear it manually via Settings → Apps → VoltGuard → Clear Data.
5

Advertising (Google AdMob)

VoltGuard displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support free access to the app. AdMob may use device identifiers to show relevant ads.

  • AdMob may collect the Android Advertising ID (GAID) for ad personalization
  • AdMob may use device information such as model and OS version
  • Your battery data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser
  • VoltGuard does not pass any personal or battery data to AdMob

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information on how Google uses data in ads, please visit: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

VoltGuard Pro (paid upgrade) removes all advertisements entirely.

Children's Privacy

Data Security

Since VoltGuard does not transmit any data externally, the primary security boundary is your device itself. All locally stored data is protected by Android's application sandbox — other apps cannot access VoltGuard's private database.

We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date to maintain the latest security protections.

9

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Revoke any permission at any time via Settings → Apps → VoltGuard → Permissions
  • Clear all locally stored data via Settings → Apps → VoltGuard → Clear Data
  • Export your battery history as CSV from the Settings screen
  • Uninstall the app to permanently delete all stored data
