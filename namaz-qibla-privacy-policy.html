Section 01

Overview

Namaz Time & Qibla ("the App") is developed by Zavi Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

By using this App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

Our Core Principle This App is designed to work fully offline. We do not operate any server that receives or stores your personal data. Your prayer times, location, and tracker data stay on your device only.
📋
Section 02

Data We Collect

The App collects only the minimum information needed to calculate accurate prayer times and provide its features. All data is stored locally on your device.

📍
Location (Latitude & Longitude)
Used solely to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Stored on your device only via Android DataStore. Never transmitted to any server. You may enter your location manually instead of using GPS.
🌆
City & Country Name
Derived from your GPS coordinates via Android Geocoder for display purposes only. Stored locally. Not shared with anyone.
⚙️
App Settings & Preferences
Your chosen calculation method, Azan sound, reminder times, language preference, and prayer enable/disable toggles. Stored locally on your device only.
🕐
Prayer Tracking Data
Records of which prayers you have marked as completed or missed. Stored locally in Room database on your device. Never uploaded or shared.

Section 04

App Permissions Explained

The following Android permissions are requested by the App. Each permission is used only for its stated purpose.

Permission Why It's Needed
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 To detect your GPS coordinates once for calculating accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Not used continuously. You can skip this and enter your city manually.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show Azan alerts and prayer reminder notifications at prayer times. Required on Android 13+.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
USE_EXACT_ALARM		 To trigger Azan notifications at precise prayer times. Without this, alarms may be delayed by Android's battery optimization.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 To play the Azan audio reliably in the foreground without being interrupted by Android's background process limits.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To reschedule prayer alarms automatically after the device is restarted, so you never miss an Azan.
VIBRATE To provide vibration feedback in the digital Tasbeeh counter feature.
WAKE_LOCK To ensure the Azan plays even when the screen is off or the device is in sleep mode.
INTERNET Required only to load advertisements from Google AdMob. Prayer time calculations work fully offline.
Accelerometer & Compass sensors To power the live Qibla compass screen. Only active when the Qibla screen is open.

Section 05

Local Storage

All data saved by this App is stored locally on your device using Android's built-in secure storage systems. No data leaves your device to any server operated by Zavi Studio.

Android DataStore (Preferences) Stores your settings: location coordinates, city name, calculation method, Azan sound preference, reminder minutes, and language selection.
Room Database (SQLite) Stores prayer time cache for the current day and your prayer tracker records (which prayers you marked completed or missed).
How to delete all data Go to Android Settings → Apps → Namaz Time → Storage → Clear Data. This permanently removes all locally stored data. Uninstalling the App also removes all data.

Section 07

Advertising (Google AdMob)

This App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this App or other websites and apps.

AdMob may collect the following information independently:

📱
Advertising ID (GAID)
A resettable anonymous identifier used for ad personalization.
🌐
IP Address & General Location
Used to serve geographically relevant ads. Not precise GPS location.
📊
Ad interaction data
Whether an ad was viewed or tapped. Used to measure ad performance.
How to opt out of personalized ads Go to your Android device: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete Advertising ID (Android 12+) or opt out of Ads Personalization (older Android). You can also visit adssettings.google.com to manage your preferences.
Our Ad Placement Policy We never display ads on screens showing Quranic text, prayer content, Duas, or the Qibla compass. Ads are shown only on the Home screen and Settings screen, never during any religious content.

Section 09

Your Rights & Choices

Since all data is stored locally on your device and no personal data is collected by our servers, you have complete control:

🗑️
Delete your data
Clear App data via Android Settings, or simply uninstall the App. All locally stored information is permanently deleted.
📍
Revoke location permission
Go to Android Settings → Apps → Namaz Time → Permissions → Location → Deny. You can still use the App by entering your location manually.
🔔
Disable notifications
Turn off Azan and prayer reminder notifications from within the App's Settings screen, or from Android Settings → Apps → Namaz Time → Notifications.
📢
Opt out of personalized ads
Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
GDPR / CCPA Since we do not collect, store, or process personal data on our servers, formal data subject requests (access, deletion, portability) are handled entirely by deleting the App from your device. For AdMob-related data requests, contact Google directly at policies.google.com/privacy.
