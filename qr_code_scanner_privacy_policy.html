Privacy Policy

QR Code Scanner & Maker · Zavi Studio
Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short
  • QR codes and barcodes are scanned and decoded on your device. Camera images are never uploaded or stored.
  • Your scan history, created codes, favorites and notes are saved only on your device.
  • We don't have user accounts, and we don't collect or sell your personal information.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers to serve and measure those ads.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app QR Code Scanner & Maker (package name com.scriptupsolutions.qrcodescanner), published on Google Play by Zavi Studio ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app handles and how.

2. Information processed on your device

DataHow it is used
CameraUsed only while you are scanning, so the app can read QR codes and barcodes. Frames are analyzed on your device by Google ML Kit's on-device barcode model and are not saved, uploaded or shared.
Images you chooseIf you scan a code from an image in your gallery, that image is read on your device to decode it. It is not uploaded.
Scan & creation history, favorites, notesStored in a local database on your device so you can view, search and export it. You can delete items at any time, or clear all data by uninstalling the app.
ContactsWhen you create a contact QR code, you can pick one contact with the system contact picker. The app reads only the contact you choose, once, to fill the form. It does not request access to your whole address book.
Wi-Fi detailsWhen you scan a Wi-Fi QR code and choose to connect, the network name and password are passed to Android as a network suggestion. They are not sent to us.
SettingsPreferences such as theme and language are stored on your device.

The content of the codes you scan or create is never sent to our servers. We don't operate any servers that receive your data. Link safety checks on scanned URLs run entirely on your device. Opening a link, dialing a number or sending a message happens only when you tap to do it, using the apps on your device.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's local data in your device backup with Google. That is controlled by your device's backup settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (you can reset it, or delete it, in your device settings)
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (for example device model, OS version, language and app version)
  • Ad interaction data (for example ad impressions and taps), plus crash and performance diagnostics for the ads

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and personalized ads

You can limit personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan codes.
  • Internet: to load ads, and to open links when you choose to.
  • Vibrate: for feedback when a code is scanned.
  • Storage (Android 9 and lower only): to save QR images you export.

5. Sharing and selling of data

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties other than as described in Section 3. Anything you export or share (for example a CSV file or a QR image) goes only where you choose to send it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy of it, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To manage data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's controls linked above.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Information exchanged with Google's ad services is sent over encrypted connections.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
