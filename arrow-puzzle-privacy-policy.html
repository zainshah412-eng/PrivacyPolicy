Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle: Tap & Clear · Last updated: February 2026

Arrow Puzzle: Tap & Clear ("the App") is developed by Zavi Studio. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

Gameplay data (stored locally only): level progress, stars earned, and daily-challenge streak are stored on your device using Android's DataStore. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or any server, and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the App.

Advertising data (via Google AdMob): the App shows banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between levels, and an optional rewarded video ad to continue after running out of lives. To serve and measure these, Google's AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, IP address, general location derived from IP)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completion)

This collection is performed by Google, not by us directly. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device's system settings.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties except as necessary for Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.

Data Retention & Deletion

All gameplay progress is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data. We do not retain any copy of it, because we never receive it.

Permissions Used

  • Internet / Network State — required so the AdMob SDK can request and display ads. Not used for any other purpose.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
