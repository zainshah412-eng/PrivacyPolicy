1. Introduction

2. Information We Access

The App requests access to the following data on your device:

Data Type Why We Need It Uploaded?
Contacts (names, phones, emails) To backup, organize, and manage your contacts ❌ Never
Storage (read/write) To save backup files locally on your device ❌ Never
Internet To display ads (Google AdMob only) Ad data only

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Contact data is accessed only on your device to provide app functionality.
  • Backup files are saved locally in your device's internal storage.
  • We do not transmit, upload, sync, or store your contacts on any server.
  • We do not analyze, process, or read your contact data for any purpose other than displaying it to you within the app.
  • No account or login is required. We have no user database.
📢

4. Advertising (Google AdMob) Third Party

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve relevant ads. This includes:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, OS version, and app usage data
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google's data practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Your contact data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser.

You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting Google Ad Settings or through your device's Privacy Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

💾

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All backup files are stored in your device's private internal storage — not accessible by other apps.
  • No data is stored on our servers — we have none.
  • If you uninstall the App, all App data including backup files is deleted from your device.
  • We recommend keeping your device secure with a PIN or biometric lock to protect your backup files.
🔑

6. App Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose
