Privacy Policy — RiseUp: Alarm Clock & Timer

Publisher: Zavi Studio  •  App package: com.zavistudio.riseup
Last updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zavi Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile application RiseUp: Alarm Clock & Timer (the “App”). By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

Summary: RiseUp does not require an account and does not collect personal information to run its alarm, timer, and stopwatch features. Your alarms and settings are stored only on your device. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects limited device and advertising data as described below.

1. Information We Collect

Data you create in the App (stored on-device only). Alarms, timers, labels, sounds, and preferences are saved locally on your device. We do not upload, sync, or transmit this data to our servers — we do not operate servers that receive your alarm data.

Advertising data (collected by Google AdMob). The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and, where applicable, an app-set ID;
  • Approximate, non-precise device information (device model, OS version, language, coarse region derived from IP);
  • IP address;
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (impressions, clicks, crashes related to ad rendering).

This processing is performed by Google as an independent controller/processor under its own terms. See Google’s policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2. Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

Where required by law (for example under the GDPR), the App presents a Google-certified consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch so you can accept or decline personalized advertising. You can review or change your choice at any time from the App’s Privacy options control. If you decline consent, ads may still be shown but will be non-personalized.

3. Device Permissions and Why We Use Them

  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarm — to ring your alarms at the exact time you set, even in low-power (Doze) states.
  • Notifications — to display the ringing alarm and its full-screen alert.
  • Run at startup (boot completed) — to re-schedule your alarms after the device restarts so they are not lost.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — to reliably keep the alarm sound playing while an alarm is going off.
  • Vibrate — to vibrate the device for alarms, when enabled.
  • Internet & network state — required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads.

These permissions are used only for the purposes above. The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

4. How Information Is Used

On-device data is used solely to provide the App’s features. Advertising data is used by Google to serve, cap, and measure ads and to combat fraud and abuse. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not share on-device alarm or settings data with anyone. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google (AdMob), for the advertising purposes described above.

6. Data Retention

On-device data remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google’s own retention policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Security

Because alarm and settings data stays on your device, its security depends on your device’s own protections (lock screen, encryption). We rely on Google’s security measures for advertising data.

9. Your Choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Change your personalized-ads consent from the App’s Privacy options control.
  • Revoke any permission in Android Settings → Apps → RiseUp → Permissions (note: some alarm features may stop working correctly).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, within the App or store listing.
