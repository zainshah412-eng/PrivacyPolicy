PixShrink Photo Compressor

Privacy Policy

Effective: May 2025

🔒Our Core Commitment

PixShrink compresses photos entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone — including us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information PixShrink collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. By using PixShrink, you agree to this policy.

📸Your Photos & Files

PixShrink accesses your photos solely to compress them locally on your device. We do not:

  • Upload your photos to any server
  • Store your photos outside your device gallery
  • Transmit photo content over the internet
  • Share your photos with third parties
  • Use your photos for training, analytics, or any other purpose

Compressed images are saved directly to the Pictures/PixShrink folder in your device gallery. All processing happens entirely offline using Android's built-in image engine.

📊Information We Collect

PixShrink collects minimal information to operate the app:

  • Total bytes saved and photos compressed (stored locally on your device only)
  • Compression history (stored in local Room database on your device only)
  • App preferences such as default compression mode (stored locally via DataStore)

None of this information is transmitted to us or any third party. It exists only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

📡Third-Party Services

PixShrink uses the following third-party services which may collect limited data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertisements. May collect device identifiers and usage data for ad targeting. You can opt out via your device's ad settings.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and subscriptions. Collects purchase transaction data required to validate subscriptions.
  • Google Play Services — required for app distribution, in-app reviews, and core Android functionality.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services directly.

🎯Permissions We Request

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — to let you select photos for compression from your gallery
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to run batch compression as a background notification so it continues while you use other apps
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show compression progress in the notification bar (Android 13+)
  • INTERNET — required only by AdMob and RevenueCat for ads and purchase validation

We do not request CAMERA, CONTACTS, LOCATION, MICROPHONE, or any other sensitive permission.

👶Children's Privacy

PixShrink is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

🔄Data Retention & Deletion

All data stored by PixShrink (compression history, statistics, preferences) is stored locally on your device. You can:

  • Clear compression history from within the app (History screen → Delete All)
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → PixShrink → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data

We have no server-side data to delete because we never receive your data.

🌍International Users

PixShrink is developed in Pakistan and is available globally on the Google Play Store. Since all processing happens on your device and we do not collect personal data, there is no cross-border data transfer by our app. Third-party services (AdMob, RevenueCat) may transfer data internationally subject to their own policies.

📝Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of PixShrink after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

We will notify users of material changes through an in-app notice.
