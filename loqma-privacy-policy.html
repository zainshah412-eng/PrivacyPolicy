Privacy Policy

Effective: July 1, 2026 Version 1.0 com.zavistudio.loqma

Plain-language summary: Loqma is a calorie and macro tracker built for South Asian cuisine. Your food logs, body metrics, and health data are stored only on your device. We do not sell your data, serve ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing. The only external services we contact are Open Food Facts (to look up packaged food) and Google Gemini AI (to identify unknown barcodes) — both are called on your behalf and receive no personally identifying information.

01

Information We Collect

Loqma collects only what is necessary to calculate your nutrition targets and track your daily food intake. Nothing else.

Data Why we need it Where it lives
Name Personalise your dashboard greeting On-device only
Age, gender, height, weight Calculate BMR, TDEE, and daily macro targets using Mifflin-St Jeor formula On-device only
Activity level & fitness goal Adjust calorie and macro targets to your lifestyle On-device only
Meal logs & food entries Track daily calorie and macro intake On-device only
Water intake logs Track daily hydration toward your goal On-device only
Barcode scans Look up packaged food nutrition via Open Food Facts or Gemini AI Sent to API (barcode only)
Camera Scan product barcodes — never stored or transmitted as images Not stored
We do not collect your email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, IP address, or any form of analytics or crash reporting data.
02

How We Use Your Information

Your data serves one purpose: making Loqma work for you.

  • Calculate personalised calorie and macro targets from your body metrics
  • Display your daily food and water logs on the dashboard
  • Show weekly trends and progress in the Trends tab
  • Send water intake reminders via local notifications (no server involved)
  • Look up nutrition data for barcodes you scan
Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or sold to any third party. We do not operate any servers that store your personal information.
03

Data Storage & Security

All personal data is stored locally on your Android device using the following mechanisms:

  • Room Database — meal logs, water logs, and food cache stored in an SQLite database within the app's private storage directory
  • DataStore Preferences — your profile, body metrics, and settings stored in the app's private DataStore
  • Android's private app storage — inaccessible to other apps without root access

No data is transmitted to our servers because we operate no servers. If you uninstall Loqma, all your data is permanently deleted from your device by Android's standard app removal process.

Backup: If you have Android Auto Backup enabled in your device settings, app data may be included in your Google account backup. This backup is encrypted and controlled entirely by Google's backup infrastructure — Loqma has no access to it.
04

Third-Party Services

Loqma makes outbound network requests to exactly two external services, and only when you trigger a specific action:

Service When contacted What is sent Their policy
Open Food Facts When you search for packaged food by name or scan a barcode Barcode number or search query text only. No user identity. openfoodfacts.org/privacy
Google Gemini AI When a scanned barcode returns no result from Open Food Facts Barcode number only, with a structured prompt. No user identity, no images. ai.google.dev/terms

No analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.), no advertising SDKs, and no crash reporting services are included in Loqma.

When Loqma contacts Open Food Facts or Gemini, your name, body metrics, meal history, and all personal data remain entirely on your device. Only the minimum information needed to answer your search query is transmitted.
05

Health Data & Android Health Connect

Loqma optionally integrates with Android Health Connect to read and write nutrition and hydration records. This integration is optional and requires your explicit permission grant during setup.

  • Loqma may write nutrition records (calories, protein, carbs, fat) and hydration records to Health Connect when you log a meal or water intake
  • Loqma may read existing records to avoid double-counting if you use other health apps
  • Health Connect data is governed by Google's Health Connect Privacy Policy
  • You can revoke Loqma's Health Connect permissions at any time in your device's Health Connect settings

Your health data stored in Health Connect is controlled by you and Android. Loqma does not transmit Health Connect data anywhere.

06

Children's Privacy

Loqma is designed for users aged 13 and above. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

If you believe a child under 13 has used Loqma and entered personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action, which may include providing guidance on uninstalling the app and clearing local data.

07

Your Rights & Data Control

Because all your data lives on your own device, you have full control at all times:

  • Access: All your data is visible directly within the app — your dashboard, meal logs, water logs, and profile settings
  • Edit: Update your profile, body metrics, and goals at any time via the Settings screen
  • Delete: Uninstalling Loqma permanently removes all locally stored data from your device
  • Portability: We are working on a data export feature (CSV/PDF) for a future release
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke camera permission or Health Connect access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Loqma → Permissions
Since we hold no data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Your data exists only on your device and optionally in your Google account's encrypted Android backup.
