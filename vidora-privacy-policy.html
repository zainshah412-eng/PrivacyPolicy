Privacy Policy

Vidora: Video Player All Format
Package: com.zavistudio.vidora
Developer: Zavi Studio
Effective date: 15 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 15 September 2026

Vidora is an offline video player. It plays video files that are already on your device. We do not operate an account system, we do not upload your videos, and we do not build a profile of what you watch.

The short version

  • Your videos never leave your device. Vidora has no upload, sync or cloud feature. We never transmit, copy or read the contents of your media files off your phone.
  • We never send file names, folder names or file paths anywhere. This is enforced in the app's code, including in crash reports.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • Vidora shows ads through Google AdMob, and (unless you turn it off) collects anonymous, aggregate usage statistics. Both are described in detail below.

1. Information Vidora stores on your device only

The following stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

  • Playback history and resume positions — which videos you have watched and where you stopped, so "Continue watching" works.
  • Favourites — videos you have marked.
  • Settings — theme, subtitle appearance, playback speed, sort and view preferences, background-playback and sleep-timer settings.
  • Private folder contents — videos you move into the in-app private folder, along with your PIN.

Private folder security

Videos you add to the private folder are stored inside the app's own private storage and are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is generated on your device, wrapped by a key held in the Android Keystore, and never leaves your device. Private folder files are excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Your PIN is stored as a salted hash, not as readable text.

Important: because the key is bound to your device's Keystore, we cannot recover private-folder videos for you. If you uninstall the app, wipe the device, or clear the app's data without first unhiding your videos, those videos cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

3. Advertising

Vidora is free and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. Vidora shows banner ads and native ads placed between items in your video list. Ads never appear over the video player and never interrupt playback.

To serve ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate (IP-based) location, device model, operating system version and ad interaction data. This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Vidora shows a consent dialog (Google's User Messaging Platform) on first launch where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising, and you can change that choice later from Settings → Privacy options. On any device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, from your Android system settings.

Vidora never sends file names, folder names, file paths or anything about your video library to advertising networks.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

Vidora uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to find bugs.

Analytics is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → Analytics. When it is off, no analytics events are sent.

The events we record are deliberately coarse. They tell us that a feature was used, never what you watched. Examples: a video started playing, a video finished, picture-in-picture was used, subtitles were enabled, background playback was used, the sleep timer was used, a private-folder feature was used. Attached details are limited to values such as whether the video came from the main library or the private folder, a resolution bucket (for example "1080p"), and rounded durations.

Enforced in code: Vidora is built so that no file name, display name, folder name, relative path, subtitle file name, MediaStore URI or MediaStore ID can be attached to an analytics event, and nothing at all about the contents of the private folder is ever reported. Crash reports are filtered the same way: exception messages that could contain file paths are stripped before the report is sent, leaving only the error type and the stack trace.

Firebase processing is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials — Vidora has no sign-in.
  • We do not collect the contents, names or locations of your video files.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, messages, call logs, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

6. Data retention and deletion

All Vidora data stored on your device — history, favourites, settings, PIN and private-folder files — is removed when you uninstall Vidora or clear its data from Android Settings. You can also clear playback history from within the app.

Advertising and analytics data held by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier at any time from Android Settings, which disassociates future ad data from past data. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your device, or to ask any question about this policy, write to zavistudioinfo@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Vidora is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Vidora plays whatever video files are already on the device; it does not provide, stream or recommend any content of its own. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, to object to processing, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. Because Vidora holds no account data, most of your data is under your direct control on your own device. For requests relating to advertising or analytics data processed by Google, contact us at zavistudioinfo@gmail.com and we will assist you.

9. Security

App data is stored in Android's private per-app storage, which other apps cannot read. Private-folder videos are additionally encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a Keystore-backed key. All network traffic generated by the app (ads, analytics, crash reports) uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not hold your media on any server, so there is no central store of your videos to breach.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Vidora handles data, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use Vidora after an update means you accept the revised policy.
