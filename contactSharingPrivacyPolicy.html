01

Overview

Contacts Transfer ("the App") is a peer-to-peer contact sharing application developed by Zavi Studio. It allows users to transfer contacts directly from one Android device to another using local Wi-Fi (LAN) discovery, a local-only Wi-Fi hotspot, or a QR code connection — without routing contact data through any server. Transfers are end-to-end encrypted between the two devices.

The App is free and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, the role of our advertising partner, and the guarantees we make regarding your data. By using the App, you agree to these terms.

03

How a Transfer Works

All data exchange is fully local between two devices. The protocol is as follows:

1
Device discovery — The other device is found via local Wi-Fi (LAN) discovery, a local-only hotspot, or by scanning a QR code. No contact data is shared at this stage.
2
Encrypted handshake — A direct connection is opened between the two devices on the local network. Both sides exchange keys and derive an end-to-end encryption key (ECDH → AES-GCM); each phone shows a short matching verification code.
3
Approval prompt — The receiver sees the sender's name and contact count and must explicitly accept before any contact data is sent.
4
Transfer — Contact data (name, phone numbers, emails, organisation, job title, addresses, websites, events, notes and photo) is sent directly to the receiver over the encrypted local connection.
5
Save & disconnect — The receiver saves the contacts to their address book and the connection is closed. The contact data is never sent to us or any server.

On devices without Wi-Fi hardware (for example some tablets, Chromebooks or desktops), the Wi-Fi and hotspot options are hidden and you can instead use Export/Import (vCard, .vcf). Exported vCard files are created on your device and only leave it if you choose to share them yourself; importing reads a .vcf file you select. This happens entirely on-device and no contact data is sent to us or any server.

04

Data We Do Not Collect

We (Zavi Studio) do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our own servers:

Your contacts or contact data · Your device's GPS location · Account credentials of any kind · A copy of your transfer history

Your contacts move only between the two phones you choose and are never uploaded to us. The only data leaving your device is handled by our advertising partner, described below.

05

Local Transfer History

The App maintains a local transfer history stored in a Room database on your device. This log records the date, direction (sent/received), the other device's name, the number of contacts, and the names of transferred contacts for each completed transfer.

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time via the History screen. Uninstalling the App removes this database entirely.
