Privacy Policy

CalcMate – Smart Calculator  •  Zavi Studio
Last updated: August 20, 2026
The short version: CalcMate works offline. Your calculations, history, favorites, and settings stay on your device. We do not require an account and we do not collect, sell, or upload your personal data.

1. Who we are

CalcMate – Smart Calculator (“the app”, “CalcMate”) is developed and published by Zavi Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle. If you have questions, contact us at zavistudioinfo@gmail.com.

2. Information the app collects

CalcMate is offline-first. All calculations run on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on our servers. Specifically:

  • Calculations & history: Your expressions, results, calculation history, favorite tools, and preferences are stored only in local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Account: None. CalcMate has no sign-in and no user profile.
  • Personal data: The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

3. Advertising

CalcMate may display ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google’s advertising SDK may collect and process certain information, such as a device advertising identifier, general (coarse) device information, and ad-interaction data. This processing is governed by Google’s own policies. You can learn more and manage your choices here:

  • Google’s Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in your device’s Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where required, ads are requested in a non-personalized manner.

4. Analytics & crash reporting

If enabled in a given build, CalcMate may use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand which features are used and to diagnose crashes. Any such data is aggregated and anonymous and never includes the values you calculate (for example, a loan amount or a GPA is never sent). You can turn analytics off inside the app where the option is provided.

5. Permissions

CalcMate requests only the minimum permissions needed for the features above:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only to load ads and, if enabled, analytics/crash reporting. Calculations do not need the internet and work fully offline.

CalcMate does not request camera, location, contacts, microphone, phone, or external storage permissions.

6. Data retention

Data stored locally (history, favorites, settings) remains on your device until you clear it in the app (Settings → Clear calculation history) or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.

7. Children’s privacy

CalcMate is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where ads are shown to audiences that may include children, they are handled in line with Google Play’s Families policy and served in a non-personalized manner.

8. Your choices

  • Clear your calculation history any time in the app.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy? Email zavistudioinfo@gmail.com.
