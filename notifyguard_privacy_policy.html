Privacy Policy — NotifyGuard

Publisher: Zavi Studio  ·  App: NotifyGuard (com.zavistudio.notifyguard)
Effective date: 23 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 23 August 2026

The short version. NotifyGuard is built to be private by design. Your notifications — their titles, text, and the apps they came from — are processed and stored only on your device. We never receive them, never upload them, and never sell or share them. The only information that ever leaves your device is the limited data Google's advertising and consent services need to show ads, which you control (details in Section 6).

1. Who we are

NotifyGuard is developed and published by Zavi Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what NotifyGuard does and does not do with your information. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Our privacy principle: local-first

NotifyGuard is an offline, on-device notification manager and history log. Everything the app does with your notifications happens locally, inside the app's private storage on your phone. There is no NotifyGuard account, no cloud sync, and no NotifyGuard server that receives your notification data.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store on our servers the content of your notifications (titles, messages, sender names, or the apps they came from).
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • We do not access your contacts, call logs, photos, files, precise location, or microphone.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Information processed on your device

To do its job, NotifyGuard reads and stores the following on your device only:

DataWhyWhere it lives
Notification content (title, text, app package, timestamp) To build your notification history, apply your rules, and power search and digests Local encrypted-at-rest app database on your device
Your rules, filters, and app preferences To mute, prioritize, highlight, or group notifications the way you set up Local app storage (DataStore) on your device
App settings (theme, retention period, lock, etc.) To remember how you configured the app Local app storage on your device
Encrypted backup files you choose to create Only if you use the Backup feature; the file is written where you choose and is protected with a password you set (AES-256) Wherever you save/share it — it never comes to us

This data stays on your device unless you deliberately export or share a backup file. If you turn on automatic retention, older notifications are deleted automatically after the period you choose.

5. Permissions we request and why

PermissionPurpose
Notification access (Notification Listener) The core permission. It lets NotifyGuard see incoming notifications so it can log, filter, and manage them. This access is used entirely on-device. You can revoke it any time in your phone's Settings.
Post notifications To show you digests, re-alerts, and highlighted notifications you asked for.
Run at startup / background work To keep logging and to deliver scheduled digests after your phone restarts.
Biometric / device credential Only if you enable the in-app lock, to protect your history behind your fingerprint, face, or device PIN.
Internet & network state Used only to request and display ads and to fetch the ad-consent form. It is not used to transmit your notification data.

6. Advertising

The free version of NotifyGuard shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process a limited set of data, such as a device advertising identifier, coarse (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, and ad-interaction events. This is standard for AdMob-supported apps and is governed by Google's own policies, not by us.

  • Your notification content is never used for ads and is never shared with Google or any ad partner.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, and other applicable regions): On first launch we show Google's certified consent form (User Messaging Platform). Your choice determines whether you see personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Your controls: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can change your consent choice at any time from the app's privacy options.
  • Learn how Google uses this data: Google Privacy & Terms — How Google uses information from partner apps.

7. Analytics

We may collect a small amount of anonymous, aggregated diagnostic and usage data (for example, which screens are opened, crash reports, and ad-performance counts) to keep the app stable and improve it. This data is not tied to your identity and never includes the content of your notifications. Where the app offers an analytics toggle in Settings, you can turn this off.

8. How your information is shared

We do not share your personal or notification data with third parties. The only third party involved in data processing is Google, solely for the advertising and consent functions described in Section 6, and any anonymous analytics/crash reporting in Section 7. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the ad personalization you consent to.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • You are in control. You can delete individual notifications, clear an app's history, or clear all history from within NotifyGuard at any time.
  • Automatic retention deletes older notifications after the period you set.
  • Uninstalling NotifyGuard permanently deletes all of its on-device data, including your history, rules, and settings.
  • Because we never receive your notification data, there is nothing about it for us to retain or delete on our side.

10. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. NotifyGuard also offers optional protections you can enable: a biometric/PIN app lock, screenshot and recents-screen protection, hiding sensitive notification content, and password-based AES-256 encryption for any backup file you create.

11. Children's privacy

NotifyGuard is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is rated for a general audience.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data and to opt out of certain processing. Because your notification data lives only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (view, edit, delete, and export your data). For any request relating to advertising or analytics data processed by Google, please use the controls in Section 6 or contact us below.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. Continued use of NotifyGuard after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
