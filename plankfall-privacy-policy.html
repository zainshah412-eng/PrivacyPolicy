🔩 Plankfall: Nuts & Bolts Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Plankfall is an offline puzzle game by Zavi Studio. You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or other personal details. This policy explains the limited data the game and the Google services it uses may process.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters and settings are saved on your device. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include this game data (the game's settings and its own database) in your device backup. We can't see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad between some levels, and reward videos only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), basic device information and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Usage analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find bugs and see which levels are too hard, the game sends gameplay events (for example: level started, level completed, boosters used) to Google Firebase Analytics, and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. They are linked to a random app-instance identifier and, where allowed, the advertising ID, plus device information, never your name or contacts. You can turn usage analytics off in Settings > Usage analytics; crash reports are still sent so we can fix crashes.

Rating the game

The Rate button opens the game's page on Google Play. Your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game schedules a local notification on your device (on Android 13 and later, Android asks for your permission first). Nothing is sent to us.

What we don't do

We don't sell personal data, and the game has no in-app purchases. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files, and it has no chat or social features.

Children

Plankfall is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google AdMob and Firebase is kept under Google's own retention rules. To ask a question or request deletion of data linked to your app instance, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date above.
