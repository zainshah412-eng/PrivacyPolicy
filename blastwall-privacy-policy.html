🧱 Blastwall: Pixel Shooter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Blastwall: Pixel Shooter ("Blastwall", "the app") is a puzzle game published by Zavi Studio. You can play without creating an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. This policy explains what data is processed when you play, by whom, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Zavi Studio does not run its own servers for this game and does not collect personally identifiable information. Your game data stays on your device (see section 5). Some third-party services built into the app, all provided by Google, process limited device and usage data as described below.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blastwall is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad between some levels (never during play, and not in the first levels), and reward videos that play only when you tap an offer that names its reward. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • The device advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system, language and network type
  • Ad interactions (impressions and clicks) and app diagnostics

Consent: where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the game.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most devices). Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

3. Analytics and Crash Reports (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. This may include:

  • Game events such as levels started, completed or failed, and boosters used
  • Session length and frequency
  • Device model, operating system version and app version
  • Crash logs and performance data
  • An app-instance identifier generated by Firebase

This data is not used to identify you. You can turn analytics off at any time with the Analytics switch in the game's Settings. Crash reports are still sent when the app crashes, so we can fix stability problems; they contain technical information only.

4. In-App Purchase (Google Play Billing)

The game offers one optional purchase, Remove Ads, processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase is owned, so it can turn off banner and between-level ads. Reward videos stay available as an option after purchase.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device:

  • Levels cleared, stars and your picture album
  • Coins, boosters and daily reward progress
  • Settings such as sound, vibration, theme, reduced motion and reminders

This data is not sent to us. Your level progress database is excluded from Android cloud backup; your settings may be included in your device's own Google backup if you have backup turned on. Local data is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

6. Notifications

Blastwall can send one optional daily reminder about your daily reward. It is off until you turn it on, and on Android 13 and later the system asks for notification permission at that moment. Reminders are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn the reminder off in Settings → Game, or in your device's notification settings.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load ads, check purchases and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Vibration — for optional haptic feedback while you play.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — only if you turn on the daily reminder.
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob as described in section 2.
  • Billing — to offer the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage or precise location access.

8. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics — Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play In-App Review — Google Privacy Policy

9. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone other than the Google services listed above, which process it under their own policies. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data on your device is kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data held by Google services is kept under Google's retention policies. Because we do not hold personal data on our own systems, there is no account to delete; you can still contact us with any request and we will help you with the Google controls that apply.

11. Children's Privacy

Blastwall is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Your Choices and Rights

  • Change ad consent at any time: Settings → Privacy options (where available)
  • Opt out of personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Turn analytics off with the Analytics switch in Settings
  • Turn the daily reminder off in Settings or your device's notification settings
  • Delete all local game data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us and we will help.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
