Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · Applies to Cutora for Android (com.zavistudio.cutora)

The short version: your photos never leave your phone. Cutora removes backgrounds using processing that runs entirely on your device. There are no servers, no uploads, no account, and no sign-in. The app shows ads, and Google collects an advertising ID to do that — that is the only data anything here collects.

Who we are

Cutora is developed and published by Zavi Studio. In this policy, "the app" means Cutora for Android, and "we" and "us" mean Zavi Studio.

Your photos

Cutora removes image backgrounds using on-device processing. The photos you open, the cutouts you create, and the images you export are handled entirely on your phone.

We do not upload your images. We do not transmit them anywhere. We do not store them on any server, and we have no way to see them. The app contains no code that sends image data off the device.

  • Photos you open are chosen by you through Android's system photo picker, so the app only ever receives the specific images you select. It cannot browse your photo library.
  • Saved projects are kept in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you delete the project in the app, or when you uninstall the app.
  • Exported images are written to your device's own gallery, in a folder named Pictures/Cutora, where you control them like any other photo.

What is collected

The app displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID. This is done by Google rather than by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your advertising choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be asked about personalised advertising the first time you use the app. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID from Android's own settings, under Privacy or Google → Ads.

Internet access

Cutora needs an internet connection the first time you use it, so that Android can download the image-segmentation model it uses to detect subjects. After that download completes, background removal works completely offline.

The app also uses the internet to load advertising. All network traffic uses HTTPS; unencrypted connections are disabled.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos and media — through Android's system photo picker, so only the images you pick are shared with the app.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only) — to save finished images to your gallery. Not requested on Android 10 or later.
  • Notifications — to show progress while a batch of images is processed in the background. Optional; declining it does not stop batch processing.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads and download the segmentation model.

Keeping and deleting your data

Because nothing is held on a server, there is no account to close and no data for us to delete on your behalf. You are always in control:

  • Delete a saved project in the app to remove its stored copy. Deleting a project never touches the original photo in your gallery.
  • Uninstalling Cutora removes all of its saved projects and settings from your device.
  • Exported images live in your gallery and are deleted the same way as any other photo.

Children

Cutora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes — for example, if image processing ever moved off the device — would be described here before the release that introduced them.
