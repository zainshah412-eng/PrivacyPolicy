Privacy Policy

SonicLift: Volume Booster & EQ · Zavi Studio
Effective date: 7 October 2026

In short: SonicLift never records, listens to, or reads what you play. It has no microphone access and no accounts. Your sound settings stay on your phone. To keep the app free, it shows ads (Google AdMob). It also uses Google Firebase to count which features get used and to report crashes. You can turn off usage analytics in Settings.

This policy explains what information the SonicLift Android app ("SonicLift", "the app") handles, why it handles it, and the choices you have. SonicLift is published by Zavi Studio ("we", "us").

1. What SonicLift does not collect

  • No audio. SonicLift applies Android's built-in audio effects (volume boost, equalizer and bass) to your phone's sound output. It does not record, capture, analyse or transmit any audio, and it does not request microphone permission.
  • Nothing about what you listen to. SonicLift does not know which app, song, video or call is playing.
  • No account or personal details. There is no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or location.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following data is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us:

  • Your boost, equalizer and bass settings, and which preset is active.
  • Custom presets you create, including the names you give them.
  • Device profiles: the type of audio output (for example speaker, wired headphones or Bluetooth) and, when Android provides it, the product name of the connected audio device. SonicLift uses this to apply the right sound automatically when you switch outputs.
  • App preferences such as theme, haptic feedback, "restore last settings" and your analytics choice.

This data is deleted when you uninstall SonicLift or clear its storage in Android Settings.

4. Your choices

  • Usage analytics: SonicLift → Settings → Usage analytics. Turning this off stops Firebase Analytics collection. Crash reporting stays on, because we need it to keep the app stable.
  • Ad consent: if consent applies in your region, you can review or change it at any time in SonicLift → Settings → Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by device).
  • Notifications: you can allow or deny SonicLift's notification at any time in Android Settings.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and whatever you include only to reply to you.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Google keeps analytics, crash and advertising data for the periods set out in its own policies and settings. To ask for any data connected to your use of SonicLift to be deleted, email us at the address below. Your app instance ID makes this easier, but it is not required.

8. Security

All network traffic from SonicLift uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't access.

9. Children

SonicLift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to how it is used. To exercise any of these rights, contact us. We will respond within the time the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app's Play Store release notes.
