RotateX — Rotation Control
Privacy Policy

Developer: Zavi Studio · Effective date: 2 October 2026

In short
  • RotateX has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, photos, files, location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • Your rotation settings and per-app rules are stored on your device.
  • If you turn on per-app rotation, RotateX checks which app is open on your device only. That information is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • RotateX does not use an Accessibility Service and never reads what is on your screen.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

This policy explains what information RotateX — Rotation Control ("RotateX", "the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android app published by Zavi Studio on Google Play.

1. Information stored on your device

RotateX saves the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your selected rotation mode and whether RotateX is on
  • Per-app rules you create (an app's package name and the orientation you chose for it)
  • App preferences such as theme, vibration, restore-after-restart and temporary-mode timers

We do not receive this information. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your Google account, under Google's own backup terms. Uninstalling RotateX deletes this data from your device.

2. Permissions and how they are used

PermissionWhy RotateX needs it
Display over other appsRotateX places a small, invisible window that asks Android to use your chosen orientation while other apps are open. It shows no content and cannot read what is on screen.
NotificationsShows the persistent notification you use to change or stop the rotation mode.
Foreground serviceKeeps your chosen orientation active while you use other apps.
Usage access (optional)Requested only if you turn on per-app rotation. Used to tell which app is currently open, so the matching rule can be applied.
Run at startupRestores your rotation mode after the phone restarts, if you turned that setting on.
VibrateOptional confirmation tick when the mode changes. Off by default.
Internet / network stateUsed to load ads and, where enabled, anonymous analytics and crash reports.

Per-app rotation and usage access

When per-app rotation is on and you have at least one rule, RotateX checks which app is in the foreground, compares it with your rules, and immediately discards it. RotateX does not keep a history of the apps you use, does not build usage statistics, and never sends this information off your device. Detection pauses while your screen is off and stops entirely when you turn per-app rotation off. You can revoke usage access at any time in Android Settings.

List of apps

To let you choose apps for rules, RotateX displays the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon. This list is read on your device for display only and is never transmitted.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

RotateX is free and supported by banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, app and ad interaction data, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, RotateX asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice from the app's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings › Google › Ads (wording varies by device).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand which features are used and to fix crashes, RotateX may use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These can collect app interaction events, device and OS information, app version, an app-instance identifier and crash diagnostics.

  • Analytics records that a feature was used — for example, that a per-app rule was added — but never which app the rule is for.
  • Analytics data is not used to identify you and is not combined with advertising data by us.

Learn more: Privacy and security in Firebase.

5. Google Play services

RotateX uses Google Play's in-app review and in-app update features. These are provided by Google Play and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not see your review account details.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use an Accessibility Service, read screen content, or record your activity in other apps.
  • We do not access your files, photos, camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not require you to create an account.

7. Data retention and deletion

Settings stored on your device remain until you clear the app's data or uninstall RotateX. Data collected by Google for ads, analytics and crash reports is retained according to Google's policies. To ask about data associated with your use of RotateX, email us at the address below; because we hold no account or contact details for you, we may need you to identify your device's app-instance in order to help.

8. Security

Data sent by the Google services above is encrypted in transit. Local settings are kept in RotateX's private app storage, which other apps cannot read.

9. Children

RotateX is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us to exercise them. You can also use the in-app ad privacy options and your Android privacy settings described above.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will show the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
