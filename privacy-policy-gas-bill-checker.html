Privacy Policy

Gas Bill Checker

By Zavi Studio  ·  Effective June 17, 2025

📋 Overview

Gas Bill Checker ("the App") is developed and maintained by Zavi Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it to check utility bills from SNGPL, SSGC, PTCL, Nayatel, and StormFiber.

We built this App as a convenience tool. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

✅

Short version: We do not collect your name, email, location, or identity. The consumer number you type stays on your device. We do not operate any backend server.

🚫 Information We Do Not Collect

Zavi Studio does not collect, transmit to our servers, or retain any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your device ID, IMEI, or hardware identifiers
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your consumer/account/reference numbers entered in the App
  • Bill contents, payment history, or any financial data
  • Browsing history or usage analytics beyond what is described below

⚙️ How the App Works

When you enter a consumer number, the App makes a direct network request from your device to the relevant utility company's public website (e.g. sngpl.com.pk, viewbill.ssgc.com.pk) using the same URL you would open in a browser. The App acts as a browser wrapper.

  • Bill data is fetched directly from the utility company's servers — not routed through Zavi Studio systems
  • Downloaded bill PDFs are saved temporarily to your device's internal cache only and are not uploaded anywhere
  • Cached PDFs are stored in your device's app cache folder and can be cleared at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Gas Bill Checker → Clear Cache
  • Bill history labels you create are stored locally on your device using Android's local database (Room) and never leave your device

🌐 Internet Permission

The App requests the android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. These are used solely to:

  • Fetch bill information from utility company websites
  • Download bill PDFs to your device cache
  • Load and display advertisements via Google AdMob

No other network communication is made by the App itself.

📢 Advertising & Your Choices

Gas Bill Checker is a free app supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob and may be personalized based on your interest profile. You can control ad personalization:

  • Android 12+: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (disables personalized ads across all apps)
  • Android 11 and below: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Ads will still appear but will be non-personalized if you opt out

🔒 Data Security

Since we do not collect or store your personal data on our servers, there is no data at risk on our end. Bill PDFs downloaded to your device are stored in Android's private app cache directory, which is not accessible to other apps without root access.

We use HTTPS for all network requests. Note that SNGPL's servers use an expired SSL certificate — the App accepts it to maintain functionality, consistent with how a browser would behave when you click "Proceed anyway."

👶 Children's Privacy

Gas Bill Checker is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
