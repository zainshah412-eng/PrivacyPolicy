Privacy Policy — SoundNova: Decibel Meter

Zavi Studio · Effective 10 September 2026 · Last updated 10 September 2026

SoundNova measures how loud your surroundings are. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your microphone and your data.

The short version. SoundNova never records, saves, or uploads audio. Sound from your microphone is analysed in memory, moment by moment, and discarded. Only the resulting numbers — decibel readings — are ever kept, and they stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them yourself.

1. Who we are

SoundNova is developed and published by Zavi Studio. For any question about this policy or your data, contact zavistudioinfo@gmail.com.

2. Your microphone

SoundNova needs the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) to measure sound levels. Despite the name Android gives this permission, the app does not record anything.

  • Audio is read in short blocks, converted into a decibel value, and immediately discarded. Nothing is written to storage.
  • No audio file is ever created, kept, transmitted, or sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The microphone is used only while a measurement is actively running. Leaving the app stops the measurement and releases the microphone.
  • SoundNova does not listen in the background and has no background listening mode.

3. What is stored on your device

When you choose to save a measurement, SoundNova stores the following locally, in the app's own private storage:

  • Start and end time, and duration.
  • Calculated decibel values: minimum, average (Leq), maximum (Lmax) and peak (Lpeak).
  • A compact list of whole-decibel readings taken during the measurement, used to draw the chart. These are numbers, not audio.
  • The frequency weighting used and your calibration offset.
  • Any name or note you type yourself.
  • Your app settings and preferences.

This data never leaves your device on its own. You can delete an individual measurement at any time, clear all measurements from Settings → Data, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

4. Sharing and exporting — only when you ask

SoundNova can export a measurement as a CSV file or share it as an image. This happens only when you tap Share or Export, and the file goes wherever you send it using Android's own share sheet. Zavi Studio receives no copy.

5. Analytics

SoundNova uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used, so the app can be improved. Events are anonymous and describe actions, never content — for example that a measurement was started, or that the analyser screen was opened.

  • No audio and no raw sound data is ever sent to analytics.
  • Decibel readings are not sent. Some events include a coarse category such as "quiet" or "loud", and a duration in whole seconds.
  • You can turn analytics off completely in Settings → Analytics. The choice is remembered and applies from that moment on.

6. Crash reporting

SoundNova uses Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes so they can be fixed. A crash report contains technical information such as the device model, Android version, and where in the code the failure happened. No microphone data and no measurement data is attached to crash reports.

7. Advertising

SoundNova is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Ads appear on the History, Settings and Noise Guide screens only — never over the meter, the analyser, the controls, or a permission request.

  • Google may use a device advertising identifier to select and measure ads. Google's own handling of that data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are shown a consent form before personalised ads are used, through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice later from Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can limit ad tracking system-wide in your Android settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

8. Permissions and why each one exists

PermissionWhy SoundNova needs it
RECORD_AUDIOTo read sound levels from the microphone. Audio is analysed in memory and never saved.
VIBRATETo vibrate when a noise alert you configured is triggered.
INTERNETTo load ads and send anonymous analytics and crash reports. Measuring itself works fully offline.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATETo check whether a network is available before attempting an ad request.

SoundNova does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, files, camera, phone, or SMS, and does not collect any of them.

9. Data we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, precise or approximate.
  • No contacts, messages, call logs, photos, or files.
  • No audio recordings of any kind.

10. Children

SoundNova is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

11. Your rights and choices

  • Analytics: turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Ad personalisation: change your consent choice in Settings, or limit ad tracking in your Android settings.
  • Your measurements: delete them individually or all at once in the app.
  • Everything: uninstalling SoundNova removes all app data from your device.

Because SoundNova has no account and stores no personal data on any server, there is no server-side profile of you for us to export or erase. If you have a request or a question about your data, write to zavistudioinfo@gmail.com and we will respond.

12. A note on measurement accuracy

SoundNova is not a certified or laboratory-grade sound level meter, and its readings must not be used for legal, occupational-safety, regulatory or medical purposes. A phone microphone is designed for speech, and its sensitivity varies by device, by model, and with any audio processing the manufacturer applies. Readings are a useful relative measurement and a reasonable estimate once calibrated — not a certified measurement.
